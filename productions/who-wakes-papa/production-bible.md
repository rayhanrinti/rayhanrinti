## 1. Video Concept

**Logline:** On a sleepy Sunday morning, 10-month-old Noor wakes up first and makes it her mission to wake Papa. Papa is only pretending to sleep, and the game becomes the warmest moment of the family's week.

**Theme:** The best mornings are the slow ones you spend together.

**Emotional arc:** Calm wonder → playful mischief → belly-laugh joy → funny "bed takeover" → tender stillness.

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** A golden sunbeam crosses a quiet bedroom and lands on a sleeping baby's face.

**End line (on screen):** *"Some mornings are made of love."*

**Why it works for short-form:** A clear goal is set up in under 10 seconds. A payoff beat lands every 4–5 seconds (hair stroke, beard pat, fake snore, crooked glasses, airplane lift, tickle, bed takeover). There is no dialogue, so it plays in any language. The last wide shot matches the first, so the video loops.

## 2. Story Summary

**Beginning (0:00–0:18):** Dawn light spills into a cozy bedroom where Mama, Noor and Papa are asleep. Noor wakes and strokes Mama's hair. Mama smiles in her sleep and rolls away, so the mission moves to Papa. Noor crawls across the blanket toward him.

**Middle (0:18–0:47):** Noor pats Papa's beard. Papa answers with a huge fake snore, and Noor dissolves into giggles. Papa secretly opens one eye. He's in on the game. Noor grabs his glasses off the nightstand and wears them crooked. Papa "wakes" with a theatrical gasp and lifts her into a gentle airplane. Mama wakes to the laughter and joins in with a tickle attack.

**End (0:47–1:07):** Time-skip: later, during the morning nap, Noor sleeps sprawled like a starfish across the whole bed. Mama and Papa are squeezed onto the edges and swap a silent, amused look. Noor's eyes open in a big macro close-up. She sees them and smiles, and both parents lean in to kiss her cheeks. The camera pulls back to the glowing window.

### SCENE 1 — "Golden Quiet" · 0:00–0:04 (4s)

- **Lighting:** Golden window key (≈3200K), warm lamp practical, lavender shadows in the corners.
- **Audio:** A single soft piano note, faint birdsong, quiet room tone.

## 9. Editing Instructions

**Software:** CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro. Timeline: 1080 × 1920, 24 fps.

### Assembly

1. **Pick takes:** Generate 3–4 video takes per scene. Choose the take with the most stable face, then trim the first and last ~6 frames, where AI clips tend to warp.
2. **Consistency check:** Before locking the cut, scrub the timeline and compare Papa's beard and charcoal tee, Mama's loose hair over her right shoulder and lavender top, and Noor's plain white bodysuit. These are the exact things the reference video lets drift. Regenerate any shot that drifts.
