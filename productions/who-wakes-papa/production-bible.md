# "Who Wakes Papa?" — 3D Animated Family Short

**Production Bible · v1.0**
Format: 9:16 vertical · 1080 × 1920 · 24 fps · ~63 seconds · 12 scenes
Style: High-end cinematic 3D, Pixar-inspired family aesthetic, warm golden-hour lighting

> **Reference note:** No reference video was attached to this request, so this production is an original story built to the brief: a bedroom setting, a baby with both parents, an emotion-first story with almost no dialogue, and premium 3D visuals. Once you upload a reference, the style tokens in §3 and the camera language in §4 can be retuned to match it without rewriting the story.

---

## 1. Video Concept

**Logline:** On a sleepy Sunday morning, a 10-month-old baby wakes up first and sets out to wake Papa. Papa is only pretending to sleep, and the game turns into the warmest moment of the week.

**Theme:** The best mornings are the slow ones you spend together.

**Emotional arc:** Calm wonder → playful mischief → belly-laugh joy → tender stillness.

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** A golden sunbeam crosses a quiet bedroom and lands on a sleeping baby's face. The viewer immediately wants to know what happens when the baby wakes.

**End line (on screen):** *"Some mornings are made of love."*

**Why it works for short-form:** There is a clear visual setup in under 5 seconds. The payoff beats (the fake snore, the crooked glasses, the airplane lift) land every 5 to 6 seconds. There is no dialogue, so it plays in any language. The ending is easy to loop: the last wide shot matches the first.

---

## 2. Story Summary

**Beginning (0:00–0:14):** Dawn light spills into a cozy bedroom. Papa, Mama and baby Noor are asleep in a big bed. Papa's round glasses sit on the nightstand. Noor's eyes flutter open. She sees Papa's sleeping face, grins and crawls across the duvet toward him.

**Middle (0:14–0:45):** Noor pats Papa's beard. Papa lets out a huge, cartoonish fake snore. Noor startles, then dissolves into giggles. Papa secretly opens one eye, smiles, and shuts it again. He's in on the game. Noor grabs his glasses and puts them on crooked. Papa "wakes" with a dramatic gasp and lifts her into the air like an airplane. Noor squeals. Mama stirs, watches them, and smiles sleepily.

**End (0:45–1:03):** Mama tickles Noor's tummy, and the three of them collapse into the pillows laughing. Noor yawns, curls up on Papa's chest and drifts back to sleep, still wearing his glasses. Papa kisses her head, and Mama rests her hand on Noor's back. The camera pulls back to the window as the sunlight warms the whole room.

### SCENE 1 — "Golden Quiet" · 0:00–0:05 (5s)

- **Visual:** The bedroom at dawn. A single diagonal sunbeam cuts through the sheer curtains and catches floating dust motes. The family is asleep under the cream duvet: Mama on screen-left, Noor in the middle, Papa on screen-right. Papa's glasses are visible on the nightstand.
- **Action:** Breathing only. Noor's tiny hand twitches. The curtains drift.
- **Expressions:** Peaceful sleeping faces. Papa's mouth is slightly open.
- **Camera:** High-angle wide shot from the foot of the bed, slow dolly push-in (~10%).
- **Lighting:** Low warm sun (≈3200K key) from the window, cool blue-grey shadows in the far corners, haze in the air.
- **Audio:** A single soft piano note, faint birdsong outside, a quiet room tone.

### SCENE 2 — "Awake" · 0:05–0:09 (4s)

- **Visual:** An extreme close-up of Noor's face on the pillow. The sunbeam slides across her cheek.
- **Action:** Her lashes flutter, her eyes open and blink twice, and her gaze moves left, then right.
- **Expressions:** Sleepy confusion that turns to bright curiosity. Her nose scrunches at the light.
- **Camera:** Locked close-up at pillow level, 85mm look, with a micro push-in.
- **Lighting:** A warm rim light on her curl, a soft fill light on her face, and catchlights in her eyes.
- **Audio:** The piano melody begins (a simple 4-note motif) and a tiny baby "mm?" coo.

### SCENE 3 — "The Mission" · 0:09–0:14 (5s)

- **Visual:** From low behind Noor's shoulder: Papa's sleeping face fills the far side of the frame, his beard catching the light.
- **Action:** Noor pushes up onto her hands and knees and crawls determinedly across the soft duvet toward Papa. The duvet sinks under her hands.
- **Expressions:** Seen in ¾ as she turns: a mischievous open-mouth grin.
- **Camera:** A low over-the-shoulder tracking shot that follows her crawl. Papa's face starts soft and racks into focus.
- **Lighting:** Backlight from the window outlines her fluffy hair.
- **Audio:** The soft rustle of duvet fabric, little effort grunts, and the piano picks up a playful pizzicato.

### SCENE 4 — "Beard Check" · 0:14–0:19 (5s)

- **Visual:** A close-up insert of Papa's cheek and beard. Noor's chubby hand enters the frame.
- **Action:** She pats his beard three times, then grabs a tiny tuft. Papa's nose twitches and his eyebrow wiggles.
- **Expressions:** Papa's face stays "asleep," but an involuntary smile tug shows at the corner of his mouth.
- **Camera:** A macro-feel close-up, static, with a shallow focal plane on the fingers and beard.
- **Lighting:** Warm side light that shows the beard texture.
- **Audio:** Soft "pat-pat-pat" Foley and a tiny "ba!" from Noor.

### SCENE 5 — "The Big Snore" · 0:19–0:24 (5s)

- **Visual:** A medium two-shot of Papa lying on his back with Noor sitting beside his shoulder.
- **Action:** Papa, eyes still shut, lets out one huge exaggerated snore. His chest puffs and his lips flap. Noor jolts back onto her bottom, freezes for a beat, then bursts into belly laughter and claps.
- **Expressions:** Noor goes from wide-eyed shock to a full giggle with her two teeth showing.
- **Camera:** Eye-level medium shot with a tiny camera bump synced to the snore, then still.
- **Lighting:** Same warm key light, brighter fill to lift the mood.
- **Audio:** A comedic snore sound effect, a 0.5s music drop-out on the startle, then a burst of baby giggles as the piano and celesta return.

### SCENE 6 — "In On It" · 0:24–0:29 (5s)

- **Visual:** A close-up on Papa's face, Noor's blurred curl in the foreground.
- **Action:** Papa opens one eye slightly and peeks at Noor. His eye sparkles, he holds back a grin, and he snaps the eye shut again as she turns back.
- **Expressions:** Pure loving mischief: one raised eyebrow and a suppressed smile.
- **Camera:** A slow push-in from a medium close-up to a close-up, with a slight Dutch tilt (≤3°) for playfulness.
- **Lighting:** A strong catchlight in the peeking eye.
- **Audio:** A tiny "twinkle" chime on the eye-open and a soft muffled chuckle from Papa.

### SCENE 7 — "Little Professor" · 0:29–0:35 (6s)

- **Visual:** A low-angle hero shot of Noor sitting on the bed, the bright window behind her.
- **Action:** She holds Papa's tortoiseshell glasses (taken from the nightstand edge), clumsily pushes them onto her face upside-down and crooked, looks straight into the lens, and blinks slowly through the oversized lenses.
- **Expressions:** Serious and proud, then a wide gummy grin.
- **Camera:** A low-angle shot from duvet level, slow arc 15° to the right.
- **Lighting:** Strong golden backlight and halo, with lens reflections in the glasses.
- **Audio:** A comedic "boop" as the glasses land and a curious "ooh!" from Noor. The music adds a light whistle motif.

### SCENE 8 — "Airplane!" · 0:35–0:40 (5s)

- **Visual:** A medium-wide shot. Papa sits up with an exaggerated gasp.
- **Action:** Papa does a theatrical "who are you?!" double-take at the bespectacled baby. Then he scoops her up and lifts her overhead in an airplane pose, gently swaying. Her arms and legs spread wide and the glasses wobble on her face.
- **Expressions:** Papa: big wide eyes, then a full laugh. Noor: an ecstatic squeal with her mouth wide open.
- **Camera:** A gentle tracking arc around the bed at chest height, following the lift upward with a slight tilt-up.
- **Lighting:** The sun flares behind Noor at the top of the lift (a soft anamorphic flare).
- **Audio:** Papa's gasp, then Noor's squeal, a soft "whoosh" for the airplane, and warm strings entering under the piano.

### SCENE 9 — "Mama's View" · 0:40–0:45 (5s)

- **Visual:** An over-the-shoulder shot from behind Mama's braid and shoulder. In soft focus, Papa holds Noor aloft.
- **Action:** Mama stirs, lifts her head slightly, and rests her cheek on her hand. Focus racks from the back of her head to Papa and Noor, then back to Mama's profile as a smile spreads.
- **Expressions:** Sleepy, then a soft, adoring smile. Her eyes are glossy with love.
- **Camera:** A static over-the-shoulder shot with rack focus, then a slight drift left to reveal her profile.
- **Lighting:** Window light from behind Mama gives her a warm hair rim and lights her freckles.
- **Audio:** A soft sleepy laugh from Mama ("mm-hmm") and the strings swelling gently.

### SCENE 10 — "Tickle Attack" · 0:45–0:50 (5s)

- **Visual:** A top-down medium three-shot. The family lies together on the pillows.
- **Action:** Papa lowers Noor between them. Mama wiggles her fingers and tickles Noor's tummy. Noor kicks her feet and grabs Mama's braid. Papa laughs with his head back, and the glasses slip down Noor's nose.
- **Expressions:** Everyone laughing openly. Mama's eyes crinkle.
- **Camera:** An overhead shot (90° top-down) with a slow clockwise rotation (~8°).
- **Lighting:** Even, bright, warm. The duvet glows.
- **Audio:** Layered family laughter (baby lead, adults under) and the music at its fullest, warm and bright.

### SCENE 11 — "Back to Sleep" · 0:50–0:56 (6s)

- **Visual:** A close-up. Noor lies on Papa's chest, cheek squished, the glasses now pushed up on her forehead.
- **Action:** Noor gives a big yawn and her eyelids grow heavy and close. Papa slowly kisses the top of her head. Mama's hand enters the frame and rests on Noor's back, wedding band visible. Noor's tiny hand curls around Papa's T-shirt.
- **Expressions:** Noor: drowsy contentment. Papa: eyes closed, deeply moved, a soft smile.
- **Camera:** A slow push-in from a medium close-up to a close-up at 85mm, very shallow depth of field.
- **Lighting:** The sun is now softer and more diffuse, and the light leans slightly peach.
- **Audio:** The music thins to solo piano. There is a tiny yawn sound and slow breathing.

### SCENE 12 — "Made of Love" · 0:56–1:03 (7s)

- **Visual:** A wide shot that mirrors Scene 1. All three are snuggled in bed, and the room is filled with golden light.
- **Action:** The curtains billow gently and dust motes drift. Nobody moves except slow breathing.
- **Camera:** A slow dolly pull-back and crane up toward the window, ending with the bed framed in the lower third and the window glow in the upper two-thirds.
- **Lighting:** The sunbeam brightens to a soft bloom. Fade to warm cream.
- **On-screen text (fade in at 0:59):** *Some mornings are made of love.* Use a rounded serif font (e.g. Fraunces / Recoleta), cream white with a soft shadow, in the upper-middle safe zone.
- **Audio:** The final piano motif resolves on the tonic, the birdsong returns, and the music tails out with a 2s reverb.

## 9. Editing Instructions

**Software:** CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro. Timeline: 1080 × 1920, 24 fps.

### Assembly

1. **Pick takes:** Generate 3–4 video takes per scene. Choose the take with the most stable face, then trim the first and last ~6 frames, where AI clips tend to warp.
2. **Consistency check:** Before locking the cut, scrub through the timeline and compare Papa's beard, Mama's braid side (her left shoulder) and Noor's yellow onesie and sun embroidery. Regenerate any shot that drifts.
3. **Upscale and smooth:** Upscale to 1080 × 1920 (Topaz Video AI, "Proteus" or "Iris" for faces). Use frame interpolation only if a clip stutters. Keep 24 fps for the film look.

### Color Grade

- **Look:** Warm golden morning. Lift shadows slightly toward teal-blue, push the midtones and highlights warm amber, and keep skin tones natural and protected.
- **Settings to start from:** Temperature +8, Tint +2, Contrast −5, Saturation 95%, Highlight rolloff soft, add a gentle Glow/Halation (10–15%) on window highlights, Film grain 8–10%.
- **Match:** Use Scene 1 as the hero grade and shot-match every other scene to it, especially skin tones across scenes.
- **Vignette:** Subtle (−10), to keep the eye centered.

### Text & Captions

- **End line:** *"Some mornings are made of love."* Fade in over 18 frames at 0:59. Hold, then fade out with the picture.
- **Optional opening hook text (0:00–0:03):** *"Who's waking Papa today? 👀"*, small, upper safe zone.
- **Safe zones:** Keep all text and faces out of the top 250px and bottom 400px (platform UI overlays).
- No dialogue subtitles are needed. The story plays visually.

### Export

- H.264 or H.265, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, VBR 16–20 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps, −14 LUFS.
- Make a 3-frame loop-match between Scene 12's last frame and Scene 1's first frame so the video replays seamlessly on Reels, Shorts and TikTok.
- **Thumbnail / cover frame:** Scene 7 (Noor in crooked glasses, low angle with a halo). It's the most clickable frame.
